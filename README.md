# Escape Velocity Simulation

This project aims to model and simulate the escape velocity of an object under the gravitational field of a celestial body using Newton's law of universal gravitation.  
The simulation explores how factors such as the mass and radius of the celestial body, as well as the object's initial velocity, affect its ability to escape the gravitational field.

## Mathematical Model

The escape velocity is given by:

$$
v_e = \sqrt{\frac{2GM}{r}}
$$

Where:

- $v_e$ is the escape velocity (m/s)
- $G$ is the gravitational constant ($6.67430 \times 10^{-11}\ \text{m}^3\text{kg}^{-1}\text{s}^{-2}$)
- $M$ is the mass of the celestial body (kg)
- $r$ is the distance from the center of the celestial body (m)

## Objectives

- Implement a mathematical model of escape velocity
- Simulate the motion of an object under the influence of gravity
- Analyze the effect of different initial velocities
- Compare the results for different celestial bodies

## Expected Results

The simulation is expected to produce visualizations showing the relationship between initial velocity, altitude, and the object's motion, allowing us to identify the conditions required for an object to escape a celestial body's gravitational field
